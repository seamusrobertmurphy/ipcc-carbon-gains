**Submission of an Original Research Paper**

Dear Editors,

Please consider the enclosed manuscript, "Soil carbon gains and offsets under updated IPCC Tier 1 methods for organic soils, coastal wetlands and improved grasslands", subtitled "Stock-change and flux-based accounting with Irish national data", for publication in Catena as an Original Research Paper.

Since 2013 the IPCC methods have allowed a country to report a soil carbon removal on rewetted peatland, restored coastal wetland and improved grassland, and no study has set those removals against the methane and nitrous oxide the same soils emit. The manuscript encodes the default emission factors and reference stocks, subtracts the emissions after rewetting from those of continued drainage across thirteen land use, climate and nutrient cases at hundred-year and twenty-year horizons, propagates the published uncertainty ranges by Monte Carlo, and tests the defaults against Ireland's country-specific factors and national soils map. It finds that the sign of the rewetting benefit is set by the horizon rather than the emission factor for five of thirteen cases, that the default reference stocks lie above the median of their own source profiles in 34 of 37 cells with a printed uncertainty that describes the precision of a global mean rather than the range a single site faces, and that Ireland's measured factors are biased in opposite directions for the two gases on the same land.

The paper belongs in Catena because its subject is the soil itself. The reference stocks are tested against the profile compilation they were drawn from, the climate-zone by soil-class stratification is examined cell by cell, and the 5 arc-minute IPCC soil classification is set against a national soils map that finds nearly three times the organic soil the global layer does. The Irish case is a test of the method rather than a regional study, since any country holding Tier 2 factors can repeat it on the same code. Every number in the manuscript is computed when the document is rendered from the encoded tables, and the source, scripts and derived data are archived with it.

The manuscript is my own work, is not under consideration elsewhere, and has not been published in any form. I am the sole author and have no competing interests to declare, and the work received no specific funding. The highlights and the declaration of competing interest are uploaded as separate files.
