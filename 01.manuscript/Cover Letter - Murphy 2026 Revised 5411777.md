**Re: New submission, a revised version of rejected manuscript 5411777**

Dear Prof. Bol,

Thank you for your decision letter of 6 August on manuscript 5411777, "Net GHG estimates using updated IPCC stock-change and flux-based methods for peatlands, wetlands and grasslands", and for the clear guidance on what a suitable revision requires. This submission is a revised version of that manuscript, and the changes respond directly to the two points in your letter.

The main text now carries eight display items in total. The version you saw carried 8 figures and 19 tables; the revision retains five tables and three figures, chosen to carry the paper's central results: the drained-minus-rewetted benefit and its Monte Carlo, the metric-dependence figure, the nitrogen crossover, the assembled pathway ledger with its duration-asymmetry figure and the map of the global soil estate the pathways apply to, and the Irish inventory sign reversal. The remaining fifteen tables and five figures now appear as supplementary material, numbered Tables S1 to S15 and Figures S1 to S5, comprising the model-input factor tables, the Monte Carlo sensitivity checks, the supporting result matrices, the global scale calculations and the data maps. The relocated items are generated by the same code as the main text and no value changed in the move.

The main text has also been shortened well beyond the ten per cent you suggested, from 9,376 to 7,957 words in the main body excluding references, a reduction of fifteen per cent that brings the manuscript within the journal's 8,000-word limit for Research Articles. The cuts fall principally in the Discussion and in the passages that had accompanied the relocated tables, and the revision has been edited throughout for a general readership, removing processing detail that belonged in the code rather than the text. No analysis, section or result was removed, and the results and conclusions are identical to the version you reviewed.

Thank you for considering the revised manuscript for the European Journal of Soil Science.
