# Highlights

- IPCC reference soil carbon stocks exceed the robust median in 34 of 37 cases
- The published error runs four times narrower than the spread of its own profiles
- Rewetting benefit turns negative in 5 of 13 cases at a twenty-year horizon
- Rewetted wetland mineral soil emits more methane than its stock change gains
- Benefit sign follows the emission metric and horizon, not the emission factor
